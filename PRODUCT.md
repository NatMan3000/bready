# Bready - Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose

A bread encyclopedia + recipe companion PWA for family use. Two jobs: (1) browse and learn about bread varieties, (2) actually bake them - phone propped on the kitchen bench, flour on hands. Built by Nathan and Josh for fun; the app should feel like it was fun to build.

## Users

- Josh and family. Non-technical, mixed ages. Kitchen context: wet/floury fingers, phone at arm's length, ambient daylight.
- Sessions are either short browsing ("what bread is this?") or long baking sessions (hours, with proofing waits).

## Brand & Tone

- Warm, playful, generous. A cookbook that likes you.
- Copy is light and a bit cheeky ("knead me!", "Fresh out of the oven!"). Never corporate, never twee-overload.
- The dough kneading toy on the home page is the personality anchor - interactive, tactile, slightly silly.

## Strategic Principles

1. **Kitchen-first ergonomics.** Big touch targets (44px min), readable at arm's length, works offline, survives a mid-bake page reload. Anything that fails with floury hands fails.
2. **Delight in moments, calm in between.** Celebrations when a bake finishes, a pop when a step is checked - but lists, filters and recipe steps stay predictable and quiet.
3. **No accounts, no cloud.** Everything local (localStorage). It's a family kitchen app, not a platform.
4. **Light and fast.** GitHub Pages static hosting; keep the bundle lean.

## Anti-references

- Recipe-blog bloat (life stories before the recipe, ads, popups).
- Generic SaaS cream/white minimalism - Bready is unapologetically warm amber.
- Gamification-heavy kids apps (points, streaks, nagging). One gentle bake counter is plenty.
